Target Stakeholder Scenarios

How our business intelligence platform addresses the exact research and commercial requirements of the three key decision-makers.

Scenario 1: Corporate EV Strategy

Emily Johnson

EV Market Strategy Director — Global OEM

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
Vehicle Portfolio & Pricing Optimization
Problem & Decision Context: Emily is tasked with allocating capital for next-generation vehicle line investments. She must know which vehicle categories drive volume versus margin, and whether high-cost battery chemistries translate into higher customer satisfaction.
1. Urban Mobility Volume

Two-Wheeler Scooters and Hatchbacks account for the highest unit sales volume globally, driven by congestion and affordability.

2. Range vs Price Sweet Spot

The 300–350 km driving range bracket yields the highest customer rating-to-price ratio for passenger sedans and SUVs.

3. Executive Recommendation

Develop modular LFP platforms for mass-market Hatchbacks and Compact SUVs to undercut legacy ICE alternatives.

Scenario 2: Automotive Market Advisory

David Chen

Business Intelligence Manager — International Consulting Firm

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
OEM Benchmarking & Market Entry Strategy
Problem & Decision Context: David advises automotive clients entering the EV ecosystem. His clients need granular intelligence on manufacturer market shares, production country advantages, and competitive gaps among market leaders like AmpereWorks and CircuitWorks.
1. Fragmented Leadership

The top 5 OEMs are separated by less than $700K in sales revenue, indicating high vulnerability to competitive disruption.

2. Production Localization

India, the United States, and China produce ~20,000 models each; entering without local manufacturing incurs prohibitive tariff penalties.

3. Client Advisory

Advise new entrants to establish joint ventures with Tier-2 battery assemblers in Southeast Asia and North America.

Scenario 3: Sustainable Transportation Research

Dr. Sarah Williams

Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst — Clean Energy Institute

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
Battery Lifecycle & Decarbonization Assessment
Problem & Decision Context: Dr. Williams evaluates environmental trade-offs between battery chemistries, vehicle weight, and long-term battery degradation to advise policymakers on clean transit subsidies.
1. Decarbonization Impact

Electric commercial buses and delivery trucks deliver 4.2x more annual CO2 offsets per kWh of battery capacity than private sedans.

2. Chemistry Degradation

LFP and Sodium-ion demonstrate superior 5-year retention rates (8.4% degradation) compared to high-nickel NCA (12.1%).

3. Policy Recommendation

Direct government zero-emission grants toward public transit electrification and sodium-ion supply chains.
